CONSTRUCTION CONTRACT AGREEMENT
===============================

Date: August 10, 2026
Project: Downtown Commercial Plaza Phase 2
Client: Apex Development Group
Contractor: CIH Build Services LLC

1. SCOPE OF WORK
The Contractor shall provide all labor, materials, equipment, and services necessary to construct the commercial plaza according to the approved blueprints (Revision 3.1) and specifications.

2. TIMELINE
Project Commencement Date: September 1, 2026
Substantial Completion Date: May 15, 2027
Final Completion Date: June 30, 2027

3. PAYMENT SCHEDULE
Total Project Cost: $2,500,000 USD
Payment will be disbursed in the following phases:
- 20% Initial Deposit upon signing
- 30% Upon completion of Foundation and Structural Framing
- 30% Upon completion of Exterior Shell and Roofing
- 20% Upon Final Inspection and Handover

4. LIABILITY & INSURANCE
The Contractor must maintain active general liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 and workers' compensation coverage for all site personnel.

5. PENALTIES
Failure to reach substantial completion by the agreed date will result in a penalty of $2,500 per day of delay, deducted from the final payment.
