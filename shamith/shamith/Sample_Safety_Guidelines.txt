SITE SAFETY & COMPLIANCE GUIDELINES
===================================
Document ID: SSG-2026-v2
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
All personnel entering the active construction zone MUST wear the following at all times:
- ANSI Z89.1 Certified Hard Hat
- ANSI 107 Class 2 or 3 High-Visibility Reflective Vest
- ASTM F2413 Certified Steel-Toe or Composite-Toe Work Boots
- ANSI Z87.1 Certified Safety Goggles (when operating machinery or in debris zones)
Failure to wear proper PPE will result in immediate removal from the site.

2. HEAVY MACHINERY OPERATION
- Only certified operators are permitted to use excavators, cranes, and bulldozers.
- A 15-foot exclusion zone must be maintained around all active heavy machinery.
- Spotters are required for all vehicles reversing in congested areas.

3. FALL PROTECTION
- Fall protection systems (harnesses and lanyards) are MANDATORY for any work being performed at heights of 6 feet or greater.
- Scaffolding must be inspected daily by a competent person before use.

4. EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS
- In the event of an accident, all work must cease immediately.
- Contact the Site Safety Manager at (555) 019-8472.
- The primary emergency assembly point is the North Gate parking area.
